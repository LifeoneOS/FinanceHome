Delete Your Account & Data

LifeOS Money · Last updated: 4 September 2026

LifeOS Money stores most of your data locally on your device by default. This page explains how to delete that data, and how to remove any additional data stored on our servers if you've used Family Sharing or Smart Split.

1. Delete everything stored on your device

In the app:

  1. Open Profile
  2. Scroll to the "Danger Zone" section
  3. Tap "Clear All My Data and Log Out"
  4. Confirm

This immediately and permanently deletes every transaction, budget, goal, habit, task, subscription, bill, categorization rule, and local profile detail (name/photo) stored on your device, and signs you out of Family Sharing/Smart Split if you were signed in. This step alone does not remove your Family Sharing profile or membership record from our servers - see the next section for that.

2. Remove your Family Sharing membership from our servers

If you've joined a Family, do this to remove your membership and profile from it:

  1. Open Family > Members
  2. Tap the Leave icon
  3. Confirm

This removes your membership and profile details from that Family's shared data on our servers (Google Firebase).

3. Request deletion of all remaining data on our servers

To request full deletion of any data still associated with you on our servers - including Smart Split history, and any Family Sharing content not covered by the steps above - email us:

lifeoscapture@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my account
Include the phone number or Google account you used with the app.

We will locate and permanently delete the associated data within 30 days of receiving your request, and confirm by email once it's done.

What's retained

Nothing beyond what's needed to process the deletion request itself (e.g. a record that a request was received and completed, containing no financial data). Crash/diagnostic logs (Firebase Crashlytics) that don't identify you personally may be retained per Google's own Crashlytics retention policy, separate from your account data.

See the full Privacy Policy for what data is collected and why.
